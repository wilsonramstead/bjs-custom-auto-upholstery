What we do

Interior work, done right the first time

From a single worn seat to a full ground-up interior, every job is measured, cut and stitched in-house. Color-matched materials, factory-grade fit, and a finish that holds up to Florida sun and daily use.

Leather Seats & Repair

Full leather seat restoration and section repairs — color, grain and stitching matched so closely you'd never know there was damage. Worn bolsters and cracked hides made right.

Custom Interiors

Complete custom interiors built to your vision — one-off patterns, two-tone layouts and custom formats. The best selection of leathers and materials to choose from.

Convertible Tops

Convertible top replacement and repair using the best materials for a clean, weather-tight fit that lasts — soft tops that look and seal like the day the car left the factory.

Headliners & Dashes

Sagging headliners re-stretched and dashes re-wrapped in fresh material — the details that quietly age an interior, brought back to crisp, tailored condition.

Classics & Restorations

Decades-old interiors returned to their original character — preserving the look and feel of the car while making every surface fresh, comfortable and show-ready.

Exotics & Special Projects

Trusted with exotic and high-end vehicles — precise, careful work on the cars owners can't afford to get wrong, delivered at a fair price versus the big-name shops.
